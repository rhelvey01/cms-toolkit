# Default Information Architecture (IA)

Derived from Cendyn's property-shot photography guidelines. Use this as the default folder
taxonomy when the client hasn't supplied their own IA. Items marked ★ are called out in the
guidelines as "strongly suggested" — treat these as the minimum bar for a category to be
considered adequately covered, and weight them higher when guessing at hero candidates.

Replace this file's structure (or point the skill at a client-supplied version) whenever a real
IA is provided — a client's own taxonomy always takes priority over this default.

## GEN — General Hotel Shots
- ★ Signature entrance & exterior (several wide shots)
- Front lobby, concierge
- Pool — wide shots & lifestyle shots
- Architecture & property details (chandeliers, ironwork, featured art, etc.)
- Cabanas
- Beachfront (with and without people)

## ACC — Accommodations
At least 3 shots per room type:
- ★ Bed type(s)
- ★ View from the room
- ★ Bathroom and/or other room highlight
- Any specific room amenities offered

## MTG — Meeting Rooms
- ★ At least 3 shots per meeting room type
- Meeting services (catering, AV equipment, in-room beverages)
- Various room setups, reception areas, staging areas

## DIN — Dining & Signature Restaurants
Per individual dining experience, at least 3 shots covering:
- ★ Exterior
- ★ Interior
- ★ Signature dishes or menu items
- Wait staff, kitchen, bar areas, atmosphere
- Views

## WED — Weddings / Events
- Ceremony, dance floor, weddings taking place on property
- ★ Wedding or event property decor
- Panoramic views of the space
- Reception areas, table setups, floral arrangements, couple arriving/departing
- Beach views, romantic sunset walks
- Couple relaxing in honeymoon suite, nostalgia shots
- Seasonal lobby setups, etc.

## SPA
At least 1 shot of each, unless starred:
- ★ Spa lobby or entrance
- ★ Spa service room(s)
- Décor / atmosphere accents
- Guests relaxing, speaking to staff
- Staff at work
- Changing rooms (if applicable)
- ★ Each type of service offered (facials, massages, manicures, etc.)

## ACT — Activities
Per individual activity area (pools, cabanas, beach, kids' room, facilities):
- ★ Property exterior (panoramic views, pools, beach views, atmosphere)
- ★ Property layout (lounge setup, chairs, locker rooms, staff pampering guests)
- ★ Property detail shot (e.g. specialty drink on bar top)
- Area attractions (recreation, watersports, golf, shopping, points of interest)
- Beach, pool, watersports
- Local restaurants, bars, golfing, hiking
- Nightlife

## LIFE — Lifestyle
Natural (not posed) shots of guests/models:
- In-room guest stay, enjoying the view
- Poolside, cabanas, lobby, workout room
- Checking in/out, arriving at property
- Staff at work
- ★ Dining — single, couple, and large groups
- ★ Shopping

## HOSP — Hospitality Services
Natural (not posed) shots of employees:
- Wait staff (delivering food, making cocktails, preparing food)
- Front desk & concierge (check-in, doorman handling bags)
- Poolside services
- Wait staff, kitchen, bar areas, atmosphere
- Room service (housekeeping, room-service food shots)

## PROMO — Special Offers / Promotions
- Family enjoying facilities
- Couples — romantic dinner, relaxing, drinking, playing
- Pets in rooms or with owners on premises
- Aerial and/or panoramic shots
- Food close-ups
- People dancing / enjoying entertainment venues

## VID — Video
- Beach activities, shopping, golfing, dining, pool scenes, local attractions/tourist activities
- See `crop-ratios.md` for placement-specific video specs (masthead, mid-page push, gallery, footer)
