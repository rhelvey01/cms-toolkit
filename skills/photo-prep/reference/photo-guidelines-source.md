# Source Guidelines (condensed)

Condensed from "Recommendation for Shooting Web-Photos for Responsive Websites" (Cendyn photo
guidelines PDF). Kept here for provenance — if a rule elsewhere in this skill seems off, check it
against this first before assuming the skill is wrong.

## Why this matters
Images that look great on desktop don't always translate to mobile — the focal point often
shifts position, making images seem out of context once reframed.

## Simple tips
- **Focal point**: shoot with the subject matter centered in the frame. The dev team orients
  images to a centerpoint that reacts to specific responsive breakpoints.
- **Plenty of room for cropping**: leave generous room around the subject so the same image can
  be cropped to different height/width ratios for different placements — it isn't always
  proportional, and the art director will often need to reuse one image across several ratios.
- **File formats**: provide high-resolution files (JPG, TIF, or RAW). Cendyn optimizes down to
  web-safe sizes in-house.

## Upload spec limits (as given by the associate, to be reconciled against final published specs)
- Max file size: 20MB
- Min dimensions: 200×100
- Max dimensions: 5500×5500
- Allowed extensions: jpg, jpeg, png, gif, svg, webp

## Property shot categories
See `ia-categories.md` for the full breakdown — General Hotel Shots, Accommodations, Meeting
Rooms, Dining & Signature Restaurants, Weddings/Events, Spa, Activities, Lifestyle, Hospitality
Services, Special Offers/Promotions, Video. The minimum is a single photo per property, but more
variety is always better; starred (★) items are called out as strongly suggested.

## Video placement specs
See `crop-ratios.md` for the placement table (masthead, mid-page push, carousel/gallery,
full-length, footer). Masthead/footer video should be ~1 minute, seamlessly loopable, no baked-in
text or sound, simple/airy shots, no sudden movements.
